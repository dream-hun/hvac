# 2. About Us

## Page Introduction

### About the Rwanda Society of HVAC and Refrigeration

The Rwanda Society of HVAC and Refrigeration is a nonprofit professional organization serving individuals and institutions involved in heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, and cooling technologies.

The Society provides a platform for technical learning, professional cooperation, industry representation, and environmental responsibility.

## Our Vision

A competent, safe, energy-efficient, and environmentally responsible HVAC and refrigeration sector in Rwanda.

## Our Mission

To strengthen Rwanda's HVAC and refrigeration sector through technical training, professional development, safe working practices, responsible refrigerant management, and cooperation between professionals and institutions.

## Our Objectives

- Improve the technical competence of HVAC and refrigeration professionals
- Promote safe installation and maintenance procedures
- Encourage responsible refrigerant handling
- Support energy-efficient cooling and ventilation systems
- Connect professionals with training and employment opportunities
- Encourage cooperation between industry and training institutions
- Increase public awareness of qualified HVAC and refrigeration services
- Represent the professional interests of Society members

## Our Values

### Professional Competence

We promote technical knowledge, practical skills, and continuous professional development.

### Safety

We support procedures that protect technicians, customers, equipment, buildings, and the public.

### Environmental Responsibility

We encourage practices that reduce refrigerant emissions, equipment waste, and unnecessary energy consumption.

### Integrity

We expect members to provide honest information, responsible services, and fair treatment to customers.

### Cooperation

We bring professionals and institutions together to address common industry challenges.

### Accountability

We support transparent leadership, responsible financial management, and measurable program results.

## Who We Serve

- HVAC technicians
- Refrigeration technicians
- Mechanical engineers
- Electrical technicians
- Students and trainees
- HVAC and refrigeration companies
- Equipment suppliers
- Training institutions
- Facility managers
- Public institutions
- Development partners

## Leadership and Governance

The Society is governed according to its constitution, internal policies, and applicable Rwandan laws.

Each leadership profile should include:

- Full name
- Position
- Professional qualifications
- Area of expertise
- Short biography
- Official photograph

# 3. Membership

## Page Introduction

### Become a Member

Join a professional community committed to improving technical competence, workplace safety, energy efficiency, and responsible refrigeration practices in Rwanda.

## Membership Categories

### Professional Membership

For technicians, engineers, consultants, installers, supervisors, and maintenance professionals.

### Corporate Membership

For HVAC companies, refrigeration businesses, contractors, equipment distributors, and service providers.

### Student Membership

For students enrolled in HVAC, refrigeration, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or related technical programs.

### Institutional Membership

For universities, technical schools, vocational training centres, public institutions, and professional organizations.

### Partner Membership

For organizations supporting technical education, environmental protection, employment, or industry development.

## Membership Benefits

- Access to technical training and workshops
- Reduced fees for selected Society events
- Access to technical guides and resources
- Professional networking opportunities
- Participation in committees and Society activities
- Information about jobs, tenders, training, and scholarships
- Listing in the member directory, with the member's consent
- Invitations to conferences and industry meetings
- Opportunities to contribute to industry discussions

## Membership Requirements

Applicants should provide:

- Completed membership application
- National identification or passport details
- Current contact information
- Professional or academic qualifications
- Employer or business information, where applicable
- Required membership fee
- Agreement to follow the Society's code of conduct

## Application Process

1. Complete the membership form.
2. Upload the required documents.
3. Submit your application.
4. Wait for the Society's review.
5. Provide corrections if requested.
6. Pay the approved membership fee.
7. Receive your membership confirmation.

## Membership Form

The form should collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Province
- District
- Occupation
- Employer or business name
- Membership category
- Qualifications
- Years of experience

- Supporting documents
